Learn AI Illustration with Phoebe

Most people who make pictures with a model have one control: the sentence they type. The stack has five layers of control and the sentence is the one with the least grip. A ratio binds. A reference binds by weight. A seed is documented at ninety-nine percent. Every word of the prompt persuades. And every character of lettering the model is asked to spell is a coin it will eventually drop, for a reason that lives in the architecture and not in the version. Six sessions on art direction with image models: where control lives, style written down, composition with a zone kept empty for type, the lettering law on mechanism, consistency as a pipeline, and the evidence for when a generated picture should not be used at all.

The six sessions 🎯 · one launch set, six decisions

The running job is Daybreak's autumn roast launch set: the coffee-subscription company from the sibling courses needs an email hero, a landing-page header, three social tiles and the printed sleeve, and the six must look like one hand made them. The sleeve carries an origin claim a lawyer signed, which is the first reason no lettering ever comes out of a model here. Every session moves the six assets one decision closer to shipping.

🎛️ Session 1 · medium

Where control lives

Five layers of control and one of them is a sentence. Which settings bind, which persuade, the vendor sentences that admit what each cannot do, and a dated control sheet for one asset.

▶ Start here 🧬 Session 2 · medium

Style DNA, written down

Six sentences a stranger could act on, including the one about what is absent. Three kinds of reference and what each carries, and a kit of four named files with one job each.

six sentences 🖼️ Session 3 · medium

Composition and the type zone

Six frames at six ratios, each with a third kept empty. Placement lines written only in words a painter could be wrong about, and the composition reference that carries shape and not colour.

the empty third 🔤 Session 4 · bench night

The lettering law

The encoder never saw a letter. The research that fixed spelling replaced the encoder, not the prompt; three vendors admit the limit in their docs. A bench reads your brief for the eleven characters that break it, and the fourteen adjectives that make it worse.

★ the bench 🔁 Session 5 · hands-on

Consistency is a pipeline

Seed at ninety-nine, style by weight, the Edit Model's four images, the adapter's five GPU hours. No vendor sells identity; the pipeline delivers it, with a person as the last step. One card on rights.

four handles ⚖️ Session 6 · hands-on

When it earns its place

Plus 19 percent clicks while nobody knows; minus 31.5 when they do; a watermark that erodes trust further. The click evidence and the trust evidence side by side, and a rule for saying no. Final scorecard.
